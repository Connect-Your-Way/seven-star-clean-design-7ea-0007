What changes the price

  • The size of the home and the number of bathrooms
  • How often we visit: weekly or fortnightly
  • Extras on a visit, such as the oven or the inside of the windows
  • Pets, and how much tidying there is before we can clean

Why the same cleaners matter

With a regular clean, the same cleaners come each visit. They learn the layout of the home, the rooms that matter most to you and anything you would rather they left alone. You know who is coming into your home, and you do not have to explain the job again each time.

Weekly or fortnightly?

Weekly suits busy households with children or pets, where floors, kitchens and bathrooms need attention often. Fortnightly suits smaller homes and households that tidy as they go. If you are not sure, tell us how the home is used and we will suggest one.

If the home has not had a thorough clean for a while, we may suggest a one-off or deep clean first, so the regular visits keep it at that standard.

What we need from you

Tell us how we get in if you will not be home, where to park, and about any pets. A quick tidy before the visit means more of the time goes on cleaning rather than moving things.
